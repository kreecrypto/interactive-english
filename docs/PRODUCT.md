# Product — Interactive English P.1

## Goal

Create an interactive English web app for Thai Grade 1 students that helps children listen, speak, recognize words and use very short English sentences through play.

## Core learning loop

1. Watch & Listen
2. Listen & Repeat
3. Practice
4. Mini Game
5. Quick Quiz
6. Reward

## Product principles

- One primary action per screen.
- Large touch targets and short instructions.
- Audio support for important English words.
- Positive feedback instead of punitive error states.
- 15–25 minute lesson target.
- Reusable activity patterns across lessons.
- Three.js only where spatial or object interaction adds learning value.

## Course scope

20 lessons covering greetings, alphabet, numbers, colors, shapes, body, family, animals, food, school, actions, feelings, weather, clothes, daily routine and review.

## MVP gate

Validate three representative lesson types before scaling to all 20 lessons:

- L01 Hello — audio/vocabulary
- L08 Colors — 3D interaction
- L12 Animals — game/quiz pattern

After the MVP passes child usability testing, freeze the reusable lesson template and scale content production.
